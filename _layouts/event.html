{%- comment -%} Layout for event pages with a public/hidden switch (first used for 2027). It is used by two kinds of pages: 1. The real pages, stored in a collection folder such as _kld2027/. They live at the usual address (e.g. /2027/), but their content is shown only when the switch `_public` in _config.yml is true. 2. Preview pages (folder previews/), whose front matter has `preview_of`. They show the same content at a secret address while the switch is false, and forward visitors to the usual address once it is true.
